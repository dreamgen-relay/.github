# Relay

**Secure business email, built for modern teams.**

Relay is a business email platform that helps organizations manage company domains, employee mailboxes, and everyday communication from one secure workspace.

It provides a fast, reliable email experience while keeping administration, access control, and domain management simple.

## What Relay Offers

- Send and receive email using company domains
- Create and manage employee mailboxes
- Organize messages, drafts, attachments, spam, and trash
- Receive real-time notifications for new messages
- Search and manage large mailboxes efficiently
- Control mailbox access with roles and permissions
- Manage multiple organization domains
- Configure authorized sending identities
- Protect business communication and account data

## Built for Organizations

Relay gives administrators a central place to manage domains, employees, mailboxes, and permissions. Team members receive a focused email experience designed for dependable day-to-day communication.

Whether supporting a growing team or managing communication across multiple business domains, Relay is designed to remain responsive, secure, and easy to operate.

## Our Priorities

- **Security** — Business messages and attachments are protected by default.
- **Reliability** — Email processing is designed to remain dependable at scale.
- **Privacy** — Access to mailboxes and organization data is carefully controlled.
- **Performance** — Large mailboxes remain fast and manageable.
- **Simplicity** — Complex email administration is presented through clear workflows.

## The Relay Mission

To give organizations a secure, dependable, and straightforward way to own and manage their business communication.
